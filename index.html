About the Artist

Drawing from the
Depths

Graphite · Pencil · Ink

These works span the sacred and the cinematic, the mythological and the deeply personal. Each piece is rendered by hand — pencil on paper — using graphite shading, controlled cross-hatching, and patient layering to build form and emotion from nothing.

From devotional portraits to surreal compositions, the art here explores what it means to see: through faith, through a camera lens, through cracked skin, or through the eye of a bird in flight.

Every drawing begins as a faint outline and becomes something that breathes — a process you can witness in the progression sketches shared throughout this portfolio.
